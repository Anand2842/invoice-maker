# Simulated e-Invoicing and e-Way Bills

The compliance workspace is at `/compliance/`. Select a business and either real or demo data. These modes are separate. The GSP adapter is a simulation: its IRNs, acknowledgement and e-Way Bill numbers and unsigned QR tokens are **not government registrations**, and no provider or government network is contacted. A real GSP can later be plugged in behind the adapter interface; credentials and live submission are not part of this implementation.

Enable e-invoicing and set the e-Way Bill threshold for the selected business and data mode. The default threshold is ₹50,000. The business's seller GSTIN and the frozen issued document data must be valid. Only issued, non-cancelled eligible documents appear. IRN generation uses the issued snapshot, so corrections should be made through the document's appropriate correction workflow rather than by altering the submitted snapshot.

Select an issued document and use **Validate IRN** to review all reported field paths before generating. The validation checks the GSTIN checksum, state and PIN, document number and date, HSN and tax arithmetic. Generate the simulated IRN after correcting any source issues. Review the recorded request, response and history on the document; each can be downloaded as JSON. The QR token is labelled unsigned and simulated. A document number already assigned an IRN cannot be reused after cancellation. Cancellation requires a reason and is limited to 24 hours after acknowledgement.

For a goods consignment, use **Generate e-Way Bill** and enter the sub-supply, mode, distance, optional transporter ID and vehicle details. Generation is allowed voluntarily at or below the threshold. Validity uses the business time zone and the distance rules for regular or over-dimensional cargo. A vehicle change requires a reason and is allowed while the bill is valid. Cancellation requires a reason and is limited to 24 hours after generation. All operations, including changes and cancellations, are retained in the simulated history. Use **Print document with compliance details** for the HTML print view.

Commands have a saved retry key scoped to the signed-in user, business and data mode. If the response is uncertain, retry the saved command before starting another one in that scope. Replaying the same command returns the recorded result; a different request cannot reuse its key. Access and writes are checked by the database against business membership; a read-only member cannot submit changes.
