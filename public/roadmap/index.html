ROADMAP

What works, and what does not yet

We label every tool with one of four words, so you never have to guess. No dates are promised; the order follows what customers ask for.

Available
Works and has been tested.
Basic
Works, with limits described below.
Practice only
A rehearsal. Nothing is sent to a government, bank or other outside service.
Coming soon
Not built yet, or waiting on a connection.

Practice only

  • e-Invoice and e-Way Bill. The steps and checks work, but the numbers and QR codes are practice values, not government registrations. A real GST provider connection is the next step.

Basic: works, with limits

  • GST returns. GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B files and a GSTR-2B check are prepared for your CA to review and upload. inbill does not file returns.
  • Bank matching. Match bank entries to payments by hand. There are no live bank feeds.
  • Items and stock. Stock movements and valuation work. Ready-made stock reports are missing.
  • Leads. Stages, follow-ups and lead to quotation. Lead forms, meetings and message templates are missing.
  • Recurring invoices and reminders. Schedules and reminders are set up in the app; sending them by email is not switched on yet.
  • TDS. Manual records and certificates.
  • All businesses and client links. One screen for accountants showing what each business owes and what is overdue, and links to invite a client. First release: no per-client GST readiness yet and no bulk actions.

Coming soon

  • Email sending of invoices and reminders. Waits on an email service being connected.
  • Online payment by card or UPI on a payment link. Waits on a payment provider being connected. UPI details already print on invoices.
  • CA view extras. GST readiness per client on the overview, and bulk actions.
  • Claude and ChatGPT connector. Create and look up bills by chatting.
  • Search across clients, vendors, items and documents, and one page of all reports.
  • Scan a bill and bulk upload from Excel on every list.
  • Day book and cash flow statement.
  • Approval steps for quotations, expenses and purchase orders.
  • Invoice editor extras: saved formats, PO number, extra charges such as freight.
  • IndiaMART lead sync and a public API.
  • Phone apps. The site already works in a phone browser.

Not planned

Payroll and HR, greetings and reviews, and a growth suite. They are different products from invoicing.
