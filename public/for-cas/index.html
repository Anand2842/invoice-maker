FOR CAs AND ACCOUNTANTS

Help your small clients invoice properly, and see where they stand

inbill is built so the accountant can open the same books the client uses. Here is exactly what works today.

Try an invoice, no account ↗ Create an account ↗

Works today

  • GST invoices done right. HSN/SAC codes, place of supply, IGST or CGST + SGST chosen from the two GSTINs, amount in words, five PDF designs.
  • Final means final. Issuing locks an invoice, so the copy the client has never changes. Corrections go through credit and debit notes; cancellations keep the original.
  • Payments and statements. Part payments, reversals, advances, receipts and client statements, with what is overdue.
  • Books that update themselves. Sales and payments post to the ledger once it is set up; trial balance, profit and loss and balance sheet read from it.
  • Returns prepared for you. GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B files and a GSTR-2B check, with a plain list of problems to fix. You review and upload; inbill does not file.
  • Several businesses, with roles. One login can open more than one business, and each can have owner, admin, editor and viewer access. An activity log records who changed what.
  • One screen for all your clients. “All businesses” shows what each owes and what is overdue, and opens any business in a click. First release: no per-client GST readiness yet.
  • Invite a client by link. They set up their business and agree on screen to give you access; you are added as an admin who can add and edit records, and they stay the owner. You send the link yourself; nothing is emailed.
  • Safe to try. One click fills a business with sample data kept apart from real records.

Not yet

  • CA view extras: GST readiness per client on the overview, and bulk actions.
  • Email sending and online payment. Not switched on yet.
  • e-Invoice and e-Way Bill are practice only. Numbers are not government registrations.
  • Filing returns. inbill prepares files; it does not submit them.
