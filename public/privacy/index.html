How inbill handles your data

What we keep

  • Your account: your email address and a password. The password is handled by our sign-in provider and is never stored by us in readable form.
  • Your business details: name, GSTIN, PAN, address, phone, email, bank and UPI details, logo, and the settings you choose.
  • The records you enter: clients, vendors, items, invoices and other documents, payments, expenses, leads and ledger entries, including the personal and business details inside them (for example a client’s name, email, phone and GSTIN).
  • Team and activity: who has access to which business, the email addresses you invite, and an activity log of changes.
  • Share and payment links you create, which show a snapshot of a document to anyone holding the link.
  • Sample data if you use it. It is kept apart from your real records.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • The site uses no advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We do not send email on your behalf yet. Emailing invoices and reminders is not switched on.
  • We do not process payments yet. Online payment is not switched on, and we hold no card or bank login details.
  • We do not file GST returns or contact any government portal for you.

Cookies and browser storage

We set two sign-in cookies, billing_access and billing_refresh, which keep you signed in and are not readable by page scripts. Your browser also stores small items for your convenience, such as unsent drafts, which menu groups are open, and whether sample mode is on. None of this is used to track you across sites.

Who handles your data for us

The site runs on Vercel. Your account, records and files are stored with Supabase. These providers may process data on servers outside India. We use them only to run the service.

If you connect an AI assistant

This is not available yet. When it is, you will choose which business an assistant such as Claude or ChatGPT can reach and whether it can only read or also create drafts. Nothing is shared with an assistant unless you agree on a consent screen, and you can withdraw access at any time. Anything you tell the assistant is handled by that assistant’s own provider under its own terms.

Your accountant or team

If you add an accountant or colleague to a business, they can see that business’s records according to the role you give them, and the activity log shows what they change. You can remove them at any time in Team.

Deleting your data

Deleting a business removes it from your account and your team straight away and switches off its share links, payment links, reminders and queued emails. Accounting records are designed to be permanent once recorded, so the underlying data stays in our database in a locked state where nobody can open it. You can ask us to erase it for good using the contact above, and we will respond.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or erase your personal data, and to withdraw consent. We aim to handle personal data in line with Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. To make a request or raise a concern, use the contact above.

Security

Access to each business is limited to its owner and the people the owner adds, enforced in the database itself. Connections use HTTPS. No system is perfectly safe, and if something that affects your data goes wrong we will tell you.

Changes

If we change this page we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you when you sign in.
